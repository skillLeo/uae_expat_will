Why R-07 is built this way

A rule that only reads the current answer cannot express “this wish is fine for one client and needs review for another”. The restricted flag never enters a notification

A capacity or influence concern raises an internal alert that says only: a case requires urgent legal attention, with its reference. It names no reason, no question and no answer.

The reason is readable only inside the case, and only by authorised legal staff. This cannot be reconfigured on this screen — it is enforced in the notification layer.

What a case coordinator cannot see

The case, its client, its status and its ordinary answers — all visible. The capacity or influence answer, its trigger reason and rule R-09 are served as “Restricted — authorised legal staff only”. The record is present and countable, never invisible, so a coordinator cannot mistake a held case for an empty one. Switch the Viewing as control in the top bar to see it.
