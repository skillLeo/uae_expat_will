Gold no longer carries meaning on paper

#AD8A46 reaches 2.87:1 on paper, below even the 3:1 non-text floor, so it cannot be an eyebrow label, an icon or a focus ring there. It is now decorative only — rules and hairlines. Functional gold is #8A6512 at 4.72:1 on paper and 5.32:1 on white. On ink, functional gold is #D8C69A at 9.32:1, because #8A6512 collapses to 2.95:1 against #12233F.

Disabled states are not signalled by fading text

Steel #B9C2D1 as a text colour is 1.79:1 on white. Disabled labels therefore stay at #5B6472 (5.98:1) and the disabled state is carried by a steel boundary, the absence of a hover response, aria-disabled and a visible reason in helper text. A greyed-out control with no stated reason is not an accessible control.

Status borders are decorative, so the icon and label are mandatory

Every semantic border sits at roughly 1.3:1 against its own fill — perceptible as an edge, useless as information. That is acceptable only because no status is ever communicated by colour: each carries a line icon and a text label, and both clear 4.5:1. This is the client's written rule and the arithmetic is what enforces it.

04
The rule system

A margin rule and a divider can never be confused

This is the cost I named in direction 1b, solved as a rule rather than a habit. Three line types exist in the entire product. They differ on all three of orientation, colour and weight at once, so no two are ever one property apart.

A fourth line — any other colour, weight or orientation — does not exist. If a screen appears to need one, it needs less content.

7.2

Registration is completed by the competent authority

Completing the online process, paying Summit's professional fee or approving a draft does not itself register a Will. Identity verification, signing, attendance and payment of authority fees remain subject to that authority's current requirements.

No document is treated as ready for approval until a legal professional at Summit Legal Consultancy UAE has reviewed it. This applies to every client, not only to matters held for review.

Why there is a fifth elevation

A shadow is cast light, so its tint has to follow the ground it falls on. --shadow-sheet at 14% of #12233F is imperceptible against #0B1729 — the card would float with no evidence of floating. The ink variant keeps the same geometry and raises the alpha to 42/32 against the deeper ink.

The section rhythm, and why

128 above each chapter, 48 between a chapter's title block and its first specimen, 80 between component groups, 64 between variants of one component, 96 before the closing block. A heading sits close to what it governs and far from what precedes it, so the page reads as chapters rather than a stack of equal panels.

128 · 48 · 80 · 64 · 96 — no value off the 4pt scale
What I removed

The spinner. Loading is a determinate 2px hairline inside the button, and the skeleton does not shimmer or pulse. A legal product that animates while a client waits for a draft looks nervous, and motion on anything a client is anxious about is the wrong instinct.
