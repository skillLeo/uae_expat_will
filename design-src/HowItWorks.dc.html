About timing

For an accepted standard matter we aim to send the first draft within 2 business days after receiving complete, usable instructions and all required documents. A matter requiring specialist analysis, further clarification or additional documents may take longer, and we will tell you if that applies.

Registration timing is set by the competent authority. We cannot guarantee that any authority will accept a Will or complete registration within a particular period.
