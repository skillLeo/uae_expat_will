Typeface note

The three directions were reviewed in Newsreader, Libre Caslon Display and Prata. Newsreader was chosen: it carries real display optical sizes at 44–72px and stays humane in a paragraph, which the product needs because the display face also sets page titles and pull quotes. Prata is the most fashionable of the three and its single weight limits the system later.
